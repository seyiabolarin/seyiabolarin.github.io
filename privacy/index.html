SITE INFORMATION

Privacy & accessibility.

A straightforward portfolio with no advertising or analytics scripts.

Contact information

The contact form sends the name, email address, subject and message you provide to Formspree for delivery to Seyi Abolarin. The site does not store submissions in its own database. You can also contact me directly by email.

Read Formspree’s privacy policy. Hosting providers and external services may process connection information as part of delivering their services.

Scheduling a conversation

If you follow a booking-calendar link, you leave this website and provide your scheduling details directly to that service under its privacy policy. No external calendar is loaded automatically on this site. Email requests use your email application and require a reply to confirm a time.

Programme information

Case studies describe professional contributions. Beneficiary datasets, household locations, personal phone numbers and home addresses are not part of this portfolio.

Accessibility

The site supports keyboard navigation, visible focus, zoom, reduced-motion preferences and layouts for small screens. Core content and links remain usable without JavaScript. If you encounter an access barrier, please email seyiabolarin@outlook.com with the page and issue.
