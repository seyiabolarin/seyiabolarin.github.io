Connecting field research with usable evidence.

ROLE Research and data management roles
PERIOD Selected work, 2016–2023

The context

Research needs careful collection, sound quality checks and findings that programme teams can use. My experience spans qualitative research, national OVC survey data management and health-intervention evaluation.

My contribution

  1. Lead data management for the national OVC pilot survey across six states, including CSPro tool development.
  2. Recruit, train and supervise 45 enumerators for WHO-supported N-PEN and NHCI evaluation in FCT, Kano and Ogun.
  3. Support translation, transcription, data cleaning, analysis, evaluation reporting and dissemination.

What the work produced

Survey instruments, coordinated data-management processes, analytical outputs and evaluation reporting.
