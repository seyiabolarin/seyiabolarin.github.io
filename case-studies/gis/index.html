GIS & OPERATIONAL PLANNING

Putting geography at the centre of health planning.

Geospatial dashboards and reference maps for service readiness, vaccination and outbreak response.

ORGANISATION Palladium · Public health programmes
ROLE Analytics, informatics & GIS
PERIOD Selected Palladium work, 2019–2025

The context

Health-service planning depends on understanding where services and needs intersect. My geospatial work supports facility monitoring, vaccination micro-planning and disease-response information products.

My contribution

  1. Develop an ArcGIS Service Availability and Readiness Assessment dashboard for Palladium IHP across three states.
  2. Create GIS-enabled information products for COVID-19 vaccination and Lassa fever monitoring in Edo State.
  3. Share practical experience with GIS micro-planning at public-health conferences.
  4. Humanitarian reference mapping. Developed geospatial reference maps and enumeration grids for DRC Nigeria, and supported DRC Cameroon with geospatial reference maps using ArcGIS and QGIS.

What the work produced

Geospatial dashboards, operational maps and conference presentations linking geographic information to programme decisions.
