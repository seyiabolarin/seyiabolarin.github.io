PERIOD Selected work, 2019–2026

The context

Information systems depend on the people who maintain and use them. My training connects data collection and quality assurance with analysis, interpretation and programme use.

My contribution

  1. Develop the national OVC data quality protocol and support training for the transition assessment.
  2. Train government M&E teams in analysis and visualization and mentor humanitarian partners in data management.
  3. Share hands-on Stata training resources covering exploration, loading, saving and data management.

What the work produced

Training delivery, protocols, reusable learning resources and ongoing technical support.
