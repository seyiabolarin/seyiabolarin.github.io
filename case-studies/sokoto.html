RESEARCH, ASSESSMENTS & GIS

Building evidence for durable solutions in Sokoto.

Profiling, labour-market assessment and spatial planning to support humanitarian decisions.

ORGANISATION Danish Refugee Council · EU SPADS
ROLE Country IM Specialist / Profiling Coordinator
PERIOD 2025–2026

The context

Durable-solutions planning needs an understanding of displaced and host communities, local livelihoods and service needs. My work connected assessment design, field coordination, data quality and analysis.

My contribution

  1. Co-design the durable-solutions workplan and questionnaire and coordinate community and government engagement.
  2. Develop enumeration grids for Sokoto and support profiling in Sokoto North, Sokoto South and Wamako.
  3. Train enumerators and programme teams; combine quantitative analysis with qualitative evidence and labour-market assessment findings.

What the work produced

Digital questionnaires, enumeration grids, field quality controls and analysis for profiling and durable-solutions planning.

FIELD / SYSTEM / DECISION
COMMUNITY GEOGRAPHY EVIDENCE
Sokoto IDP & host-community profiling

Methods & systems

Digital surveys · GIS · Atlas.ti · Quantitative analysis
