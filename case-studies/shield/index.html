SYSTEMS & INTEROPERABILITY

Connecting health, nutrition and programme systems.

Requirements, functional design and interoperability for integrated humanitarian health and nutrition systems.

ORGANISATION 3iS · ECHO SHIELDS
ROLE Information Management Professional
PERIOD July 2026–present

The context

Health and nutrition teams need consistent information across registration, referrals, follow-up, commodities and reporting. My work with 3iS supports the ECHO SHIELDS consortium in translating these operational needs into system requirements.

My contribution

  1. Map registration, case-management, referral and commodity workflows with programme teams and partners.
  2. Define functional specifications, roles, indicators, data dictionaries, validation rules and shared identifiers.
  3. Specify data exchange with ActivityInfo, DHIS2, SORMAS and partner platforms; coordinate UAT scenarios and issue resolution.

What the work produced

Functional designs for INIS, HIMS and commodity tracking; interoperability requirements; UAT scenarios; user documentation and training support.
