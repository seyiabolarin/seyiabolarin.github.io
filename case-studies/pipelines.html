DATA ANALYTICS & AUTOMATION

From field collection to programme intelligence.

Automated reporting that connects field submissions, quality checks and decision-support dashboards.

ORGANISATION Danish Refugee Council · Palladium
ROLE Information management & analytics
PERIOD Selected work, 2024–2026

The context

Programme teams need to follow field activity without repeatedly assembling reports by hand. Across humanitarian reporting and OVC data quality work, I connected collection workflows with reporting products.

My contribution

  1. Develop digital collection tools and API-connected reporting workflows.
  2. Build Kobo-to-Power BI dashboards for humanitarian programme monitoring in Borno, Sokoto, Katsina and Zamfara.
  3. Use Kobo-connected Power BI reporting to track enumerator progress during the OVC data quality transition.

What the work produced

Automated programme dashboards and operational progress reporting, supported by documented data-management practices.
