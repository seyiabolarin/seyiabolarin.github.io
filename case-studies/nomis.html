DIGITAL HEALTH SYSTEMS

Strengthening national OVC information systems.

National system development, deployment and partner reporting for orphans and vulnerable children programmes.

ORGANISATION Palladium · USAID Data.FI
ROLE Data Analytics & Informatics roles
PERIOD 2019–2025

The context

OVC programmes require dependable records and consistent reporting across implementing partners and government teams. At Palladium, I worked across system development, deployment, data quality and routine data use.

My contribution

  1. Lead NOMIS development and deployment and configure DHIS2 for OVC partner reporting across 17 Nigerian states.
  2. Coordinate Situation Room reviews and interagency reporting, and support DHIS2-to-DATIM transfer and partner harmonization.
  3. Develop data quality protocols, reporting products and training to support system adoption.

What the work produced

National NOMIS deployment support, configured DHIS2 reporting, Situation Room information products and government capacity development.

Methods & systems

NOMIS · DHIS2 · Data quality · Partner reporting

About this case study

This case study focuses on national system implementation and reporting. Patient-level records and internal programme materials are not published here.
