HUMANITARIAN INFORMATION MANAGEMENT

Making consortium information easier to use.

ActivityInfo tools, reproducible data cleaning and operational workflows for consortium teams.

ORGANISATION ECHO consortium · Danish Refugee Council
ROLE Interim Consortium Information Management Specialist
PERIOD May–July 2026

The context

A consortium staffing transition required continuity in information management and a practical handover. This assignment brought together collection tools, validation workflows, distribution lists and training.

My contribution

  1. Develop ActivityInfo collection and reporting tools and R-based cleaning, validation and deduplication workflows.
  2. Build Python scripts for distribution lists and support vulnerability scoring, referral indicators and targeting.
  3. Train teams across DRC, Solidarités International and IMPACT Initiatives in system design, user management and R–ActivityInfo interoperability.

What the work produced

Collection and reporting tools, reusable processing scripts, training and a documented handover to the incoming specialist.
