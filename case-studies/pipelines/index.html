DATA ANALYTICS & AUTOMATION

From field collection to programme intelligence.

Automated reporting that connects field submissions, quality checks and decision-support dashboards.

ORGANISATION Danish Refugee Council · Palladium
ROLE Information management & analytics
PERIOD Selected work, 2024–2026

The context

At DRC, I connected ActivityInfo and Kobo with reporting products for sector teams and management. At Palladium, I also developed Kobo-connected Power BI reporting to follow the OVC data-quality exercise.

My contribution

  1. Beneficiary targeting and automation. Developed automated Python scripts to generate beneficiary distribution lists for BHA-funded assistance, reducing preparation time. Programmed and automated scoring criteria in Kobo tools to support consistent vulnerability assessment and beneficiary targeting.
  2. Sector tools and accountability. Developed Crisis Modifier tools, multipurpose cash assistance (MPCA) tools, post-distribution monitoring tools and the Multi-Sectoral Needs Assessment (MSNA). Developed the community feedback mechanism to support accountability and programme follow-up.
  3. Integrated reporting. Connected both ActivityInfo and Kobo directly to Power BI through APIs. Built dashboards for sector teams and management, with automated refresh scheduled every six hours, daily.
  4. OVC assessment reporting. Connected KoboToolbox and Power BI to track enumerator progress during the OVC data-quality transition at Palladium.

What the work produced

API-connected ActivityInfo and Kobo dashboards with six-hour refresh schedules; reproducible processing and beneficiary-list scripts; automated scoring in collection tools; and operational progress reporting.
