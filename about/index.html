MY PATH

From biochemistry
to information systems.

My academic foundation is in biochemistry. Research work with Jhpiego introduced me to field data collection, qualitative analysis and the practical challenges of producing useful public-health evidence.

At Palladium’s Data.FI project, my work expanded into national health information systems, analytics, GIS and government capacity building. At the Danish Refugee Council, I supported a multi-donor country portfolio spanning EU SPADS, FCDO, GERF, MSF and BHA-funded work, before an additional interim NEWCAP ECHO consortium assignment. My responsibilities included profiling, livelihoods tools, cash targeting, geospatial mapping, accountability systems, reporting automation and staff capacity building.

Today, my work with 3iS centres on integrated health and nutrition systems: translating programme requirements into functional designs, connecting platforms and supporting testing, governance and adoption.
