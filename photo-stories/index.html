December 2024 · Fieldwork & teams

The people behind a data quality exercise

This group photograph marks the end of three weeks of shared effort. Alongside three colleagues, I helped guide a team of 65 enumerators through the OVC data quality transition exercise in Akwa Ibom.

Read the full story: The people behind a data quality exercise

The work brought together enumerators, community case workers, implementing partners and community-based organisation teams. We reviewed records and reports, checked consistency and coordinated the daily assessment work.

I developed a Power BI dashboard connected to KoboToolbox to help us follow the team’s progress each day. The technology supported the exercise, but the collaboration behind it is what this photograph captures: people with different responsibilities working towards a shared standard of reliable information.

September 2024 · Training & knowledge sharing

Building confidence in data quality

Ahead of the OVC programme transition, we brought government colleagues together to prepare for their role in supervising data quality assessments. The sessions combined practical guidance with discussion and hands-on exercises.

Read the full story: Building confidence in data quality

I led the development of the OVC Data Quality Assessment Protocol with my team and supported the training of Federal and State Ministry of Women Affairs staff. We focused on verification, validation and consistent reporting during the transition.

What stayed with me was the level of participation: questions, experiences from different states and a shared determination to make the exercise work. A protocol becomes useful when the people applying it understand it and have the confidence to put it into practice.

The survey behind the spreadsheets

September 2022 · Fieldwork & communities

Start by listening

Before beginning the data collection exercise, we met community leaders to explain the purpose of the visit and discuss how the work would take place. That conversation was an essential part of getting started.

Read the full story: Start by listening

Working with an interpreter, I helped explain the planned activities and listened to questions and expectations. We adapted our engagement to the community’s customs and discussed how to approach households respectfully.

The experience reinforced something I carry into systems and assessment work: good information starts with trust. Taking the time to listen, explain and agree on an approach is part of the work, not a step outside it.
